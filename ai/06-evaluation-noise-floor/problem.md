# Topic 6: evaluation, gates, and the noise floor

## The prompt

> "You published two *failing* gates on your own benchmark. Walk me through why, and tell me
> about the bug you found in your own evaluation."

## The follow-up bank

1. Why not LLM-as-judge? Everyone uses it.
2. Publishing failing gates — isn't that admitting it doesn't work?
3. `185 of 185` — what does that actually prove?
4. How do you stop the harness drifting from production?
5. Why does the noise floor matter?
6. What made you check a passing test?
7. `n/a` counted as a pass — how do you prevent that class of bug generally?
